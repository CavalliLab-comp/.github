# 🧬 Computational Biology and Integrative Genomics of Cancer

*U1331 - Computational Oncology department at Institute Curie, Paris, France*

Deciphering pediatric and adult brain tumor ecosystems through computational biology, multi-omics and spatial genomics integration. We investigate how tumor cells and their microenvironment interact across space and time to drive heterogeneity, plasticity, and treatment resistance.
